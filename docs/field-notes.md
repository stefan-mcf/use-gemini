# Field notes

These observations come from the pre-release prototype. A sample taken on 24 November 2026 covered 10 prepared jobs across five local workspaces. Seven had a valid result receipt. Four of those had an accepted review whose recorded digest matched the returned receipt. Two other jobs had a pickup marker, and one was awaiting pickup.

One exchange completed two rounds. Codex reviewed the first result, then prepared a follow-up brief linked to the first job. Antigravity returned a second receipt, and Codex recorded a review against that receipt as well. The saved files covered the path from request to follow-up without requiring either client to share its chat history.

A digest match binds a saved review to the bytes of its receipt. Independent verification comes from inspecting the actual files and rerunning the checks relevant to the brief. The sample measures saved workflow states from one operator’s local use. The installable package has its own validation instructions. [Package validation](../README.md#validation)
